ServiceNow is a cloud-based platform designed to help organizations manage digital workflows for enterprise operations. It provides a wide range of tools and services that allow businesses to streamline and automate routine processes, improving efficiency and service delivery.

Key Features of ServiceNow:
IT Service Management (ITSM):

Automates IT support tasks like incident, problem, change, and asset management.

Commonly used by IT departments to track and resolve technical issues.

IT Operations Management (ITOM):

Helps monitor and manage infrastructure, including servers, networks, and applications.

IT Business Management (ITBM):

Aligns IT services with business goals through portfolio management, demand management, and resource planning.

Customer Service Management (CSM):

Enhances customer support through case management, omnichannel engagement, and self-service portals.

HR Service Delivery:

Simplifies HR processes like onboarding, payroll, and employee inquiries.

Security Operations (SecOps):

Helps detect, assess, and respond to security threats using automated workflows.

Now Platform App Engine:

A low-code/no-code environment for building custom applications and workflows.

Who Uses It?
Large enterprises and mid-sized companies across industries like finance, healthcare, manufacturing, and government.

IT departments, HR teams, customer support centers, and developers.

Benefits:
Centralized service management.

Automation of repetitive tasks.

Improved visibility and accountability.
